# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home-insurance customers entering a residential address during a purchase flow. They need to verify that the insurer found the correct property quickly and confidently, on desktop or mobile, with full Hebrew and RTL support.

## Product Purpose

Evaluate whether a satellite-map confirmation moment makes address verification clearer and more reassuring in the home-insurance purchase flow. Success means a customer can find, visually verify, confirm, reject, and retry an address without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Reassuring, precise, and considered. The interface should feel established and human, with a restrained moment of delight when the property is found.

## Anti-references

Avoid startup landing-page theatrics, excessive animation, playful gimmicks, glass-heavy styling, and visual claims that mock property insights are real Google data.

## Design Principles

1. Make verification visual: keep the satellite view central and the address explicit.
2. Earn trust through clarity: distinguish live Google location data from illustrative mock insights.
3. Keep momentum: loading, confirmation, correction, and retry should happen in one uninterrupted flow.
4. Use motion to explain state: camera movement, marker focus, and card entry should be brief and purposeful.
5. Design RTL first while preserving natural responsive behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus, semantic form feedback, touch-friendly controls, screen-reader status announcements, and reduced-motion alternatives. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
